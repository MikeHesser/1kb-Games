1kB Games

Small Amstrad CPC games by Mike Hesser – each one not longer than 1024 bytes of BASIC. The original listings run here in the browser on a small CPC emulation.

EmergencyBomb the city to make a landing strip.
ShooterTwo players, two cannons and a mountain in between.
The towers of HanoiMove the tower, one stone at a time.
HuntingStay alive as long as possible.
MoonlanderLand the shuttle below the lunar surface.
TronLight cycle race for two players.
Source code and descriptions: github.com/MikeHesser/1kb-Games
